What is a Meto Live Host?

A Meto Live Host is a content creator on the platform, and does live streams, chats, video calls, party rooms, and other interactive features. Your job is simple: go live, keep the chat active, and give viewers a reason to come back and send you gifts.

Why Become a Meto Live Host?

You do not need to build an agency or manage a team. As a Host, your focus is on your own stream, your audience, and the interaction inside the app.

  • Easily join task, ranking, or bonus programs when they are available .
  • Withdraw eligible earnings through a safe and easy process.

Meto Live Host Rules

Following these helps protect your account and your eligibility for live features, rewards, bonuses, and withdrawals.

  • You must be at least 18 years old
  • Use only your own account and identity.
  • Do not use multiple accounts at the same time.
  • Complete required authentication and face verification honestly.
  • Do not show minors on camera.
  • Do not stream pornographic or explicit content.
  • Do not promote third-party platforms during live sessions.
  • Stay active with viewers and respond to required interactions or calls

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Meto Live Host?

A Meto Live Host is a creator who interacts with users through live streams, chats, video calls, party rooms, and other supported features inside the Meto Live App.

How do I become a Host on the Meto Live App?

Download the app, create your account, complete your profile, and finish the required verification steps. Some applicants may also be asked to complete a short audition or review process.

Do I need face verification to go live?

Yes. Host features such as live streaming may require face verification before they become available on your account.

How old do I need to be to become a Host?

Hosts must be at least 18 years old.

How can Meto Live Hosts earn?

Eligible earning routes can include live gifts, chats, private video calls, party mode, tasks, rankings, bonuses, content features, and random video matches.

Does every Host get a fixed salary?

No. Host earnings are not automatically fixed. Some Host programs may include salary or bonus opportunities, but eligibility can depend on activity, performance, account status, country, and the latest program rules.

Can I use more than one Host account?

No. Meto Live rules allow one authenticated account per user and prohibit using another person’s account or multiple authenticated accounts at the same time.

When can I withdraw my Host earnings?

You can request a withdrawal after your verified account meets the applicable minimum balance and payout requirements. Available methods and processing times can vary by country and platform policy.
