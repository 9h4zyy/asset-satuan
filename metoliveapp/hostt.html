What Is a Meto Live Host?

A Meto Live Host is a creator who connects with users through live streaming, chat, video calls, and other interactive features inside the Meto Live app.

Hosts can build exposure through attractive profiles, active interaction, consistent streaming, and good live performance. The more active and consistent a Host is, the better the opportunity to receive gifts, bonuses, and higher payout.

  • Go live through public or private live broadcasts

  • Earn from 1-on-1 messages and group chats
  • Earn diamonds from private video calls
  • Join monthly bonus and ranking reward programs
  • Sell videos, photos, or live stream recordings
  • Earn from random video match features

Audition

Host Audition Requirements

Before joining as an active Host, you may need to prepare for a trial broadcast or audition.

  • Maintain a polished, camera-ready appearance
  • Use good lighting
  • Use a stable internet connection
  • Make sure the camera quality is clear
  • Choose a quiet environment
  • Complete a trial live session for around 10–20 minutes
  • Record a 30-second video directly on the platform during the trial session
  • Send the Host's Meto ID and 30-second video to the administrator for review

The administrator will review the Host's audition and inform which Hosts have passed.

Live Streaming

Host Live Streaming Requirements

Hosts should maintain a professional, safe, and engaging live streaming environment. These requirements help improve viewer experience and protect Host eligibility for salary, bonus, and rewards.

  • Use bright lighting during live streams
  • Use a stable internet connection
  • Avoid lying down during the stream
  • Keep the live environment clean and appropriate
  • Greet users when they enter the live room
  • Thank users when they send gifts
  • Respond to incoming video calls when required
  • Keep your face visible in the camera view
  • Interact actively with viewers
  • If a Host repeatedly misses incoming video calls, salary may be canceled and the Host may be removed from the platform. Low performance or failure to meet required standards may also affect salary or rewards.
Compliance

Important Host Rules

Hosts must follow Meto Live rules to maintain account eligibility and access to rewards.

  • Hosts must be at least 18 years old
  • One user can only have one authenticated account
  • Authentication forging is prohibited
  • Using someone else's account is prohibited
  • Using multiple accounts at the same time is prohibited
  • Minors are prohibited from appearing on camera
  • Promoting third-party platforms is prohibited
  • Pornographic content is prohibited
  • Live stream violations may result in loss of daily task rewards
