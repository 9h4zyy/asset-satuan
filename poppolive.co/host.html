✦ FACE VERIFICATION

How To Complete Face Verification for Host

Face verification is required to access Host features such as live streaming. Make sure you use your Host account and follow the instructions shown inside the app.

  1. 1

    Open the Poppo Live App

    Go to the homepage. Tap the ‘Live’ button, this takes you straight into the face verification step.

  2. 2

    Upload a Photo of Yourself

    You’ll need to provide a recent, well-lit photo where your face is clearly visible. Confirm it once you’re satisfied, and you’ll move to the next stage

  3. 3

    Begin the Live Verification

    Tap ‘Start to Certificate’ to enter the real-time verification stage.

  4. 4

    Follow the On-Screen Instructions

    • Make sure you are in a well-lit room (avoid backlighting)
    • Remove hats, glasses, masks, or accessories that cover your face
    • Use the front camera in clear conditions
    • Take a selfie or record a short video on demand
  5. 5

    Submit and Check Back for Results

    • The verification process can take up to 1-3 business days.
    • If you pass, your account will officially become a verified host
    • If it fails, you can repeat the verification
